Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Leonida Daily Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Leonida Daily channel to publish videos to the channel's own accounts.

Data we process

When the channel owner connects a social account, the tool receives an access token and the account's username. These are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.

Data we do not collect

The tool has no other users. It does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other people, and it uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in the account settings of the platform. Stored tokens are then deleted.
