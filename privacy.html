Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Leonida Clipper (also called Leonida Daily Publisher) is a private tool used only by the owner of the Leonida Daily channel to publish videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

What the tool accesses through the YouTube API: with the owner's permission (OAuth scope youtube.upload) it uploads videos and their title, description and tags to the owner's own channel. It also reads public video information (title, view count, publish date) from search results to find popular gaming videos. It does not read, store or share any data about other YouTube users or viewers.

Data we process and store

When the channel owner connects an account, the tool receives an access token and the account's username. These are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos. Public video information found through search is kept in a local file on that computer for at most 30 days. Nothing is sent to or shared with third parties.

Data we do not collect

The tool has no other users. It does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other people, and it uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access for YouTube can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. Access for other platforms can be revoked in their account settings. When access is revoked, or on request by email, all stored tokens and data are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days.
